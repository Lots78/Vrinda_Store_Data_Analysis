**# **# 📊 Vrinda Store Data Analysis Dashboard (MS Excel)

An interactive **Sales Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze the sales performance of **Vrinda Store**. The dashboard provides meaningful business insights through Pivot Tables, Charts, and Slicers, helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze the **2022 sales data** of Vrinda Store and generate an interactive dashboard that helps the business understand customer behavior, identify sales trends, and improve decision-making for future growth.

## 📌 Business Questions (KPIs)

- 📈 Compare Sales and Orders using a single chart.
- 🗓 Identify the month with the highest Sales and Orders.
- 👨‍💼👩‍💼 Compare purchases made by Men and Women.
- 🚚 Analyze Order Status distribution.
- 🌍 Find the Top 10 States contributing to sales.
- 👥 Analyze the relationship between Age and Gender.
- 🛒 Identify the sales contribution of different Channels.
- 🏷 Determine the Highest Selling Category.
- ✅ Calculate the Percentage of Orders Delivered.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 👩 Women contributed approximately **65%** of total purchases.
- 📍 Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh generated the highest sales.
- 👥 Customers aged **30–49 years** placed the maximum number of orders.
- 🛍 Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra were the top-performing sales channels.
- 🚚 More than **90%** of orders were successfully delivered.

---

## 💡 Conclusion

The analysis indicates that **women aged 30–49 years** from **Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh** form the most valuable customer segment. Focusing marketing campaigns, personalized offers, and promotional strategies on this audience can significantly increase future sales.
